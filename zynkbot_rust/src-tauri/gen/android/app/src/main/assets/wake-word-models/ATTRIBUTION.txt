Wake word models bundled with Zynkbot
=====================================

melspectrogram.onnx
embedding_model.onnx
--------------------
Source:  openWakeWord — https://github.com/dscripka/openWakeWord
License: Apache License 2.0
         https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

These are the shared Google speech mel-spectrogram and speech-embedding
front-end models used by openWakeWord. They are redistributed here unmodified
under the terms of the Apache License 2.0.

hey_zynk.onnx
-------------
Source:  Zynkbot (ContainAI) — trained with wake-word-training/train_hey_zynk.py
         on a 60-voice synthetic TTS corpus, with negatives from the FSD50K
         background subset published by openWakeWord.
License: same terms as Zynkbot itself (see LICENSE in the repository root)

Why these files live here
-------------------------
They were previously fetched at runtime from a GitHub release, which meant wake
word could not be enabled without network access and tied the app to a release
that could never be deleted. Bundling them costs ~3 MB in the APK and makes wake
word work offline on first launch.

silero_vad.onnx
---------------
Source:  Silero VAD v5 — https://github.com/snakers4/silero-vad
License: MIT License, Copyright (c) 2020-present Silero Team
         https://github.com/snakers4/silero-vad/blob/master/LICENSE
A pre-trained speech-or-not detector (16 kHz, 32 ms frames). Added 2026-10-19
as a gate in front of the wake-word classifier: a detection with no human voice
in the previous two seconds is ignored. Redistributed unmodified.

generic_negatives.json
----------------------
Source:  Zynkbot — 110 false-trigger clips (noise, television, electronic sounds)
         collected on the developer's phones 2026-09-04..09-22, reduced to the
         192 pooled embedding numbers per clip. No audio, no personal voice.
Used by WakeVerifierTrainer as the shipped negative set for the on-phone verifier.
License: same terms as Zynkbot itself (see LICENSE in the repository root)
